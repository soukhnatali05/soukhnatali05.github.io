About

Security across the full lifecycle.

I'm a senior Computer Science student on the cybersecurity track at Effat University, and a published security researcher. My work spans the whole lifecycle: I simulate real-world attacks, detect them with purpose-built IDS/SOC tooling and machine learning, and translate the results into compliance against the frameworks that matter in Saudi Arabia and globally.

I'm most drawn to how attacks actually move through a network — and how to catch them. My capstone builds a full purple-team range that executes lateral movement and detects it end-to-end, then maps the telemetry to governance controls.
